Short Courses
Short courses are courses that are less than the term’s duration. Students should register for the short course no later than the add deadline for the term. These courses follow a special prorated schedule for drop and withdrawal periods.
Short courses can be located online by using the checkbox for weekend or short classes on classes.uoregon.edu .
