Accessible Formats & Technology
Services for students include:
Consultation about alternative options to access course materials
Software and hardware support for assistive technology and accommodation-related tools that assist with course and course material access
Conversion and editing of textbooks, image PDFs, and other course materials to accessible formats to fit your needs
Troubleshooting services related to any of the above
In order to use these services students must first meet with an AEC Access Advisor to determine if these services are an appropriate accommodation. If you have been approved for course materials conversion, please review instructions for Requesting Alternative Format Materials .
To request or learn more about these services, please email our front desk at uoaec@uoregon.edu .
Visit Digital Accessibility @ UO for resources for university employees related to creating and maintaining accessible digital content and services. If you have questions or need to consult with an expert, email the Digital Accessibility Architect .
