Making your Canvas Syllabus Accessible
19 November 2026 10:00am – 11:00am
Knight Library Dream Lab
Event Host : UO Online
Event Type : Workshop
Join UO Online to learn the key practices for making your Canvas syllabus page accessible. In this hands-on workshop, you will also learn how to import and customize UO’s Canvas Syllabus template from Canvas Commons and how to use the Pope Tech Accessibility Guide to catch and fix common issues. There will also be time to get one-on-one support from an instructional designer and review your Canvas syllabus page.
