Degree Level and Enrollment
Undergraduate Students
Undergraduate level courses are numbered between 100-499.
Undergraduate students who have a GPA of 3.000 may request to take a maximum of three graduate-level courses during their last three terms at the UO with approval from the instructor and the Division of Graduate Studies.
To petition, students must complete a Reservation of Graduate Credit application prior to the term of registration. If approved, the Graduate School will give the student an authorization form to share with the Office of the Registrar to complete registration.
Graduate Students
Graduate level courses are numbered between 500-799.
Graduate students should check with their graduate advisors to get permission before enrolling in undergraduate courses.
