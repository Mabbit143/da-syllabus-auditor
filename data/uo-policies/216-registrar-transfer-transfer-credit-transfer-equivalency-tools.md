Transfer Equivalency Database (TES®)
The University of Oregon has partnered with CollegeSource's Transfer Evaluation System (TES®) to provide our community with a library of our current college-level course equivalencies.
Please note that if you do not see a course listed in TES®, this does not mean it is ineligible for transfer credit. New equivalencies are added to our UO database and TES® daily as courses are evaluated. An assessment will be made for either elective or core education elective credit based on the course description in TES®. Transfer courses are assigned a smart code. More information about UO Smart Codes can be found on our site.
Our University of Oregon departments determine direct UO equivalents. Please contact an academic advisor for that department if you think one of your transferred courses is directly equivalent to a UO course.
Disclaimer
TES® Public View is updated as transfer equivalency approvals change, including the need for course reevaluation for older courses. Please contact our Transfer Team if you have specific equivalency questions.
