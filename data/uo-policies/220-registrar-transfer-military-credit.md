Military Credit Evaluation
Students who have served in the military may transfer course work for military educational experiences. The University of Oregon's policy for military credit evaluation follows the recommendations of the American Council on Education (ACE) Military Guide and follows university policies regarding transfer credits.
Transfer credits are not awarded for: Basic Training, MOS, or DANTES.
Transcripts from the Community College of the Air Force, the Defense Language Institute, Prometric and Joint Services Transcript (JST) can be sent:
By mail:
Office of the Registrar
5357 University of Oregon
Eugene, OR 97403-5257
Electronic delivery:
transfercredits@uoregon.edu
As a courtesy to members of the military, the Office of the Registrar offers unofficial evaluations of JSTs for prospective students. Students wishing for this service should request for an official JST to be sent to the University of Oregon and they should email us at transfercredits@uoregon.edu to let us know.
