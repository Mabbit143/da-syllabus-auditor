Final Exams
Final exams are scheduled based on the first hour of the first scheduled class meeting in a typical week. While our table will help you identify the date and time for your final, please confirm this is correct in your course syllabus. Law students should refer to MyLaw for the law school final exam schedule. Summer term exams are generally held on the last day of the course.
Some classes hold combined final exams which are listed on a separate schedule.
