Veterans Benefits
Your first step to receiving Veterans Benefits at the UO is to apply for VA Education Benefits online at U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs . Upon submission, you will receive confirmation that the VA has received your application. You can expect your request to be processed within 30 days. Once you have been approved for education benefits, the Certificate of Eligibility will be sent to you. Our office will need a copy of the Certificate of Eligibility.

Office of VA Education Benefits
The Office of VA Education Benefits helps eligible student veterans, reservists and dependents utilize educational benefits in compliance with the procedures and regulations of the United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA).
