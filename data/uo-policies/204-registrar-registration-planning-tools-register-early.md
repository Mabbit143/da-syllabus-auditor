Register Early
It is to your advantage to register for classes on the day you are first eligible because you will have access to the widest selection of classes. This means knowing your registration info, and being prepared to register. Find your unique registration date and time in the DuckWeb Student Menu under Check Registration Eligibility. General registration information is in the dates and deadlines calendar.
