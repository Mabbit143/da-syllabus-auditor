Pope Tech Alternative Formats in Canvas
Pope Tech's Alternative Formats feature is automatically available in all Canvas courses. It allows students to convert Canvas pages and linked files into other formats like MP3, EBook, and Word, so they can access different content options to meet their needs and preferences. You can click the Alternative Formats icon next to a content item (page, module, etc.) and be presented with a range of options for text and audio formats. You can then choose to have the converted item emailed to you or downloaded. Review Pope Tech Alternative Formats for Students in Canvas  for more information.
This built-in Canvas feature is available to all UO students, regardless of whether they have disability-related needs or are registered with AEC. Students who require alternative formats of course materials due to a disability should work directly with AEC.
