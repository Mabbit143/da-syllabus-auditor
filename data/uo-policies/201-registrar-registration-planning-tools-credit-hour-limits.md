Credit Hour Limits
Undergraduates: Limited to 18 credit hours during initial registration.
Graduates: Limited to 16 credit hours during initial registration.
Law: Limited to 17 credit hours maximum.
Non-Degree Seeking: Limited to 8 credit hours in fall, winter, and spring terms. Limited to 21 credit hours in summer quarter.
Credit hour limits increase for undergraduate and graduate students the week prior to the term starting. See the academic calendar .
