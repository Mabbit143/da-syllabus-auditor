Campus Textbook and Supplies Resources
Textbook and Course Materials Subsidy
The Textbook Subsidy is paused for fall term 2026 and will not be available. Please see below for info on other resources this term.
This subsidy helps University of Oregon students with the cost of textbooks and other academic materials that are required for the current academic term. This funding is offered on a first-come, first-served basis. Applicants must be enrolled at least half-time and demonstrate financial need. The maximum subsidy amount is $150 per academic quarter. Applications are reviewed by the Basic Needs Program and updates are communicated via email.

Sell Your Books Through the Duck Store
The Duck Store has partnered with MBS Textbook Exchange, the nation’s largest textbook wholesaler, to offer you the best prices for your used books. Shipping is free, and a shipping label will be sent to your email once your quote is finalized. This program offers in-person buyback two or three times per year.
