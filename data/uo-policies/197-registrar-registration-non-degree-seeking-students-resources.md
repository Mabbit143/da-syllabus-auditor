Resources for Non-Degree Seeking Students
Non-degree seeking students have access to a variety of resources that help them engage with the campus community.
All non-degree seeking students should obtain a student ID card, Duck ID, and a UO email address. These act as keys to campus resources and services. When wishing to use a new campus resource, any non-degree seeking student should communicate with the campus service provider or facility to understand how they may use the resource. Some courses that non-degree seeking students may take do not collect mandatory fees and thus students should do their research. Additionally, as non-degree students do not pay the health service fee, they are unable to use health or counseling services on campus.
