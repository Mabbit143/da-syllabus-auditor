Incompletes
If a student has as a circumstance arise after the withdrawal deadline in week 7 that impacts their ability to complete all course requirements by the end of the term, they may request an incomplete from their instructors.
By requesting an incomplete, the student is indicating that they plan to complete the course requirements on a modified timeline. Faculty and students must develop an agreement which outlines the requirements and deadlines for completing the work.
Incomplete Grade Request Form
No instructor is required to issue an incomplete. However, they may do so if:
the student has been active in the course
they are able to complete the remaining requirements without attending additional classes or receiving additional instruction
there were unexpected issues that occurred after the last day to withdraw from the class
Review the policy on incompletes for more details.
To request an incomplete the student must submit the incomplete grade request form before 5 p.m. on the last day of finals week.
