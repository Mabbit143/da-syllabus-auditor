How to Schedule an Appointment in Navigate
Open Navigate Student app (or in your browser) and log in with your Duck ID.
New students! You must claim your Duck ID and then have a UO email address assigned before you can successfully sign in to Navigate.
Go to Appointments > Schedule Appointment
Not sure who to schedule with or having issues scheduling? Most departments offer drop-in advising! Visit their websites for more details .
Questions? Chat with the Advising Helpdesk on Teams (requires Duck ID) or call 541-346-3211, Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm.
