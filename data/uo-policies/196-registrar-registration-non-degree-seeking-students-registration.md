Registering as a Non-Degree Student
Learners who are interested in registering for courses at the UO as a non-degree seeking students can do so by following the steps:
Fill out the Non-Degree Seeking Status form .
Obtain your student ID number and Personal Access Code via email after completing the form.
Review the UO Class Schedule.
Read and accept the university's billing account terms and conditions .
Register for courses when non-degree student registration opens.
Students who have recently registered in courses as non-degree seeking will not need to following the initial steps and can register for courses more easily.
Credits earned at the UO will appear on the official UO transcript and may be applied to a degree program.
Students who have not received a bachelor's degree will only be able to take undergraduate level courses when enrolling as non-degree seeking students.
