UO Elements
The Office of the Provost has launched the use of a new software system, Elements, designed to manage faculty reviews. This system digitizes dossiers and streamlines workflows for faculty, staff, and administrators. Elements also captures faculty achievement in the areas of scholarly and creative work, sponsored projects, teaching, and service. In AY25-26, faculty will focus on performing assigned reviews in Elements and bolstering their profiles with their CV content.
