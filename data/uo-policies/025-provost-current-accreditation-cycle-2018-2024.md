Current Accreditation Cycle (2025-2032)
The NWCCU accreditation cycle is completed over an eight-year period (new in 2026) and guided by the recently revised Standards for Accreditation. The University of Oregon initiated a new accreditation cycle in 2025 and was provided a visit schedule change letter from NWCCU to detail out our eight-year cycle .
