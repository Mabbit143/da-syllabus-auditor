Pillars of Faculty Success
Academic Excellence
Teaching | Leadership | Mentorship | Scholarship and Creative Practice
The Office of the Provost is committed to supporting faculty success in a culture of inclusive excellence grounded by the pillars of teaching, leadership, mentorship, and scholarship and creative practice. Our goal is to have a transformative impact on faculty development across the stages of their careers and provide support for faculty development and excellence for all faculty.
Teaching
The University of Oregon will provide a high-standard of teaching development, evaluation, and reward in order to create an inclusive, engaged, and research-informed culture that helps shape the student experience at UO.
