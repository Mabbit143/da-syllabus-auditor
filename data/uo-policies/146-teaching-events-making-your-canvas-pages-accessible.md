Making your Canvas Pages Accessible
18 November 2026 10:00am – 11:00am
Zoom Meeting
Event Host : UO Online
Event Type : Workshop
Join UO Online to learn the key practices for making your Canvas pages accessible. In this hands-on workshop, we will apply those practices to a syllabus page and you will learn how to use the Pope Tech Accessibility Guide to catch and fix common issues. For those interested in additional syllabus support, we will show you how to import the syllabus template from Canvas Commons. There will also be time to get one-on-one support from an instructional designer and review a Canvas page of your choice.
