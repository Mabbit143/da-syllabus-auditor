Returning Students
Students who have taken time off from the UO may need to act to continue their studies.
Undergraduate Students
Undergraduate students who have been admitted to the University of Oregon can take a break from school for less than one academic year and still register for the upcoming term. If they have taken more than one academic year away from the university, should complete the reenrollment application .
Reenrollment Application
Non-Degree Students
Students who took classes through Community Education Program (CEP) should refer to the Non-Degree Seeking program information for steps to reenroll.
Graduate Students
Graduate Students who have taken time off will need to contact the Division of Graduate Studies regarding future enrollment.
