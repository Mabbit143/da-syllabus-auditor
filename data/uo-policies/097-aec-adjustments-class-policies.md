Adjustments to Class Policies
Class policies and expectations can sometimes create barriers for students with disabilities. AEC may approve accommodations that adjust specific policies or practices to ensure students have equitable access to their courses. These accommodations are determined through an individualized, interactive process and can include (but are not limited to):
Access to food and/or drinks during class
Breaks during class
Computer in class
Permission to record lectures
Flexibility on assignment deadlines when necessary due to a disability-related event
Flexibility with attendance policy when necessary due to a disability-related event
