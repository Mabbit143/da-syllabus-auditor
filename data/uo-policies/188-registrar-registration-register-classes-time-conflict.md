Time Conflict
Undergraduate and Graduate Students
You can’t sign up for two classes that have a time overlap, e.g. one class meets every Wednesday from 8-9:50 and you want to add a course that also meets on Wednesdays that starts at 9:30. This is not allowed.
Law Students
If you’re a law student and need to register for classes that overlap, reach out to the Law School Registrar to inquire about an exception.
Request for an Exception
Consideration will be given if the overlap is 15 minutes or less or it is a single day overlap, e.g. an Outdoor Pursuits course is taking place one Friday during the term that overlaps with your Friday course that occurs every week.
Undergraduate and graduate students can use the time conflict registration request form to ask for an exception to this faculty-established policy.
Time Conflict Registration Form
Required Supporting Documentation
Before the add deadline: only instructor signatures for both classes are required.
After the add deadline: both instructor signatures and department support stamps are required for both classes and a late add petition for the course you are trying to add.
Deadline
Time conflict registration requests should be submitted as soon as the student decides they want to add the course.
Typical review/processing time: within 3 business days
Submission of a completed time conflict registration request form does not guarantee registration in the requested class(es).
Students may not attend any UO course for which they are not registered.
