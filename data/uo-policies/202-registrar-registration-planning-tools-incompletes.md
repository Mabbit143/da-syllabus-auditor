Incompletes
Do not register for any course in which you currently have a grade of "I" (incomplete). You cannot re-register for an incomplete course before the instructor’s timeframe for the removal of that incomplete.
Arrangements to remove an incomplete (grade of "I") should be made with the instructor who taught the course. If an instructor leaves the university before the end of a course, the department’s head or dean can assist with the removal.
To remove a grade of "I", the instructor must submit the grade or contact the Office of the Registrar for assistance. The instructor cannot change a grade of "I" to a grade of "W".
Incompletes that are not resolved will lapse to the default grade noted on the agreement.
