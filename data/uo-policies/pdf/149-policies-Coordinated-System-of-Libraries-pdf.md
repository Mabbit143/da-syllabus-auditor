P OLICY Coordinated System of Libraries A closely coordinated and integrated system of autonomous libraries shall be maintained. Unnecessary duplication in materials, services and procedures will be avoided. The library facilities and resources of the entire Department will be readily available to all faculty members and students of Department institutions.
