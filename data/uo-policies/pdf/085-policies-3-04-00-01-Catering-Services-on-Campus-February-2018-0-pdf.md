Catering Services on Campus Last Updated: 10/11/2000 Reason for Policy: To clarify the rules related to caterers and other groups providing food service on campus. This policy does not pertain to the sale of food and/or beverages through vending machines or by businesses that occupy leased retail spaces in University owned or leased facilities. Policy Statement: Food and Beverages served or catered on University of Oregon owned or leased property must be provided by University Catering. University Catering is the authorized caterer for events scheduled in University campus facilities. University Catering is authorized to establish additional procedures regarding operational details such as delivery, bookings, cancellations, set-up, and disposition of unused food. Beyond the exceptions listed below, individuals and/or organizations desiring to have food and/or beverages catered to approved campus locations by other food providers must receive prior written approval from the Vice President for Student Services and Enrollment Management or designee. All off- campus caterers must operate in compliance with local health department regulations and be able to provide proof of a valid business license for the specific operation, and a certificate of liability insurance. Service of alcoholic beverages at public events scheduled in University facilities must comply with UO Alcohol Service Standards and Guidelines. Copies of the guidelines are available in the Office of Risk Management. Request for Permission to Serve Alcohol Beverages form must be completed and granted prior to any event. Exceptions and Exclusions: 1. Department of Intercollegiate Athletics food service operations at sporting events. 2. Approved food vendors during the ASUO Street Faire and similar festivals. 3. Pre-packaged, non-perishable food and beverages for the consumption of department affiliated individuals only and not intended to be served or sold to the general public or greater campus community. 4. Organized student groups may prepare and serve food within University Dining (kitchen use subject to availability), under the supervision of a UO Dining provided, certified food handler with the approval of University Housing.
