The goal of this policy is to expand economic opportunities for historically underrepresented businessesHistorically Underutilized Small Businesses (HUBs) by encouraging participation in OUSUniversity contracting and purchasing. ORS 351.070(2)(c) requires the Board to adopt policies and procedures that achieve results related to the participation of Minority, Women and Emerging Small Businesses (MWESBs), as defined by the State of Oregon, that equals or exceeds the standards in effect on July 17, 1995.
