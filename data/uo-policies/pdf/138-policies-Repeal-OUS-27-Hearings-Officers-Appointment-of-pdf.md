P OLICY Should the president of the Board or the Chancellor believe that the interest of the Board would be served by having a hearing concerning the adoption, amendment, or repeal of an Administrative Rule conducted by a presiding officer instead of by the full Board, the one shall consult the other. If the president so authorizes, either of them may appoint a presiding officer to conduct the hearing. The presiding officer so designated shall conduct the hearing in accordance with the Attorney General's Model Rules of Procedure, Section 137-01-030. A report of the testimony and exhibits presented at the hearing shall be made to the Board at the time the matter is presented to the Board for action. N OTES
