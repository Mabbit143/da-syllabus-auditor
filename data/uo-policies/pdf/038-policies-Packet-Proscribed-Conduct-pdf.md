This is a revision to the Proscribed Conduct – OAR Rule 580.022.0045 to provide more clarity on appropriate use of UO facilities. This revision will provide departments with a tool to be able to ask those not engaging is appropriate use of UO facilities to leave. This also provides UOPD a clearer tool for issuing letters of trespass should that be appropriate. RELATED STATUTES, REGULATIONS, POLICIES, ETC. [Page 2]
Facility managers across campus are noting a significant increase in non-UO affiliates using UO spaces for reasons other than the university’s mission. They are also noting increased situations where these visitors are causing disruptions to the normal operations of campus. In December 2022, Safety & Risk Services convened a group of facility managers to discuss this issue, identify actions that have helped and actions that are still needed. Some facilities like the EMU and Library have building specific use policies that are used, but the institution lacked an across the board statement about the appropriate use of UO facilities. The group cited the need to either create or amend policy to provide a clearer path to address these issues. The existing Proscribed Conduct policy does address this issue, but isn’t as clear as it could be. This amendment is aimed at improving safety on campus. AFFECTED PARTIES Who is impacted by this change, and how? All persons who use University facilities. Facility Managers across campus can cite this policy when individuals are not using campus facilities appropriately. UOPD can use this policy as the basis for issuing letters of trespass.
