Faculty and Staff Resources
We believe that faculty and staff members at the University of Oregon are some of the key resources that contribute to student success. That's why we aim to provide clear and concise information to help you understand the various processes that impact student registration, academic progress, and class scheduling. Thanks for taking time to review the information as you look for ways to support student success.
