Student Experience Surveys (SES) (policy)
Beginning in Fall 2019, “Course Evaluations” were replaced by end-of-term “Student Experience Surveys”. These surveys, which are administered in most courses, ask students to report on their course experiences in terms professional, inclusive and research-informed teaching standards.
