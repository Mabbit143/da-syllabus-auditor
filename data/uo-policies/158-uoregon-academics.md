In the Spotlight
Recording mice as they hunt crickets. “Photographing” atoms without a microscope. Using electrochemistry to brew coffee with molecular precision. It’s all in a day’s work for Oregon faculty members. Learn about their recent work, in their own words.
My lab is trying to figure out how vision works, which really is asking how the brain works when we interact with the world—like reaching for a coffee cup or looking for a recognizable face in a crowd. In the past, vision studies were more like an eye exam.
Cris Niell
Professor of Biology and Neuroscience
People in the last hundred years have been building better and better lenses to look at things that are increasingly small … My work shows that you can image very small atoms without having to spend the upfront cost for an expensive microscope.
Kayla Nguyen
Assistant Professor of Physics
There's been a fad in times of trauma for soft materials. When you have a Labubu on your bag… It looks cute. You look at it, and you feel comforted.
Alisa Freedman
Professor of East Asian Languages and Literatures

Our Facilities
Museums and Libraries
Hit the books, find a quiet place to study, or explore world-class art collections.
Museum of Natural and Cultural History
Where Oregon's past is present. A place for digging into science, celebrating culture, and joining together to create a just and sustainable world.
Explore the History Museum
Jordan Schnitzer Museum of Art
Home to 20,000 works of art, the Jordan Schnitzer Museum of Art fosters global understanding of art and cultural histories to contribute to a more just, equitable, and sustainable world.
Discover the Art Museum
Knight Library
UO's largest library houses collections in the humanities, social sciences, music, and business, plus government documents, microforms, aerial photography, and maps. Knight Library also provides a variety of reservable rooms for individual and collaborative study.
Learn About the Knight Library
Allen Price Science Commons and Research Library
The PSC houses collections in the natural and physical sciences, and provides access to computers, study rooms, experiential learning spaces, a coffee shop, and additional services.
Explore the Science Library
Design Library
A center of art, architecture, and design with collections focusing on the Pacific Northwest and rare materials such as historical student architectural drawings and models, monographs, artists’ books, and reference works. The Design Library provides access to computers, study rooms, and additional services.
Learn About the Design Library
John E. Jaqua Law Library
Home to collections that support coursework, research, and instruction in law. The Law Library also provides access to computers, reservable study rooms, and numerous services.
Discover the Law Library
Mathematics Library
The Mathematics Library houses collections that support coursework, research, and instruction in theoretical and applied mathematics.
Learn About the Math Library
Special Collections and University Archives
SCUA shapes the future by preserving the past. The archive collects, catalogs, and preserves a wide range of materials, including rare books, personal and family papers, organizational archives, Oregon-related documents, visual materials, and historical records of the University of Oregon.
Explore the Archives
Portland Library
Supporting coursework, research, and teaching related to UO Portland's experiential learning programs, the library also provides access to computers and equipment, study spaces, an extensive architecture collection, a children’s library, and coffee shop.
See the Portland Library
Rippey Library, Oregon Institute of Marine Biology
A portal to the Pacific, the Rippey Library houses collections that support coursework, research, and instruction in Marine Biology and the natural and physical sciences, and study spaces, on the OIMB campus in Charleston, Oregon.
Discover the Marine Biology Library
