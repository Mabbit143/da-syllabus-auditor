Ducks on Track
Ducks on Track allows students to assess their progress toward majors, minors, and degrees. It helps students plan and consider different majors and minors.
Using Ducks on Track helps students check their progress toward their degree and plan accordingly. Along with using Ducks on Track, students should meet with their academic advisors to confirm how they are doing.
